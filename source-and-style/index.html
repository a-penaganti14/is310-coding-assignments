Bronny James: Top NIL Earner in College Basketball

This Sports Illustrated article, published in October 2023, covers USC freshman guard Bronny James topping the On3 NIL valuation rankings at an estimated $5.9 million, the highest of any college athlete at the time, despite not having played a single college game yet. His NIL value was driven almost entirely by his last name: he is the son of NBA superstar LeBron James.

This is a useful reference point for anyone studying NIL and college athletics. It shows that NIL valuation does not always track on-court production. Name recognition, family connections, and social media following can outweigh actual college performance, which complicates any research comparing NIL earnings against athletic outcomes.
