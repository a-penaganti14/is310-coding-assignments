# AT&T Stadium — Dallas Cowboys

People call this place "Jerry World." AT&T Stadium has the biggest
open indoor space of any building in the world, plus a giant video
board.

But the Cowboys are not alone in this division. Another team is
hiding nearby, and you cannot see it with a normal `ls`. Hidden
files and folders need a special command to show up.

**Next move:** run `ls -a` in this folder to find what's hidden,
then `cd` into it.
